# UI-UX-Experiment5

## Aim:
To analyze two different UX project scenarios, identify user pain points, formulate UX problem statements, select suitable research methods, and create user personas.
## Algorithm:
1. Select two UX project scenarios – Good Fit and Riders.
2. Identify target users and understand their needs and difficulties.
3. Identify user pain points for each application.
4. Write UX problem statements using the given problem-statement template.
5. Select appropriate UX research methods such as surveys, interviews, and usability testing.
6. Justify the selected research methods based on the user needs.
7. Create at least two personas for each application.
8. Include demographics, goals, behaviors, pain points, and motivations in each persona.
9. Present the findings along with the designed application interfaces.
10. Evaluate the UX 10solutions based on the identified user needs.
## Output:


SCENARIO 1: GOOD FIT – HEALTH & FITNESS APP

## **1. User Pain Points:**

  1. Users may forget to drink enough water throughout the day.
  2. Users may not know how much water they should drink daily.
  3. Users may find it difficult to track their daily water intake.
  4. Users may forget when they last drank water.
  5. Users need simple reminders to maintain regular hydration.
  6. Users may not have a clear overview of their hydration progress.

### **UX Problem Statement 1:**

Health-conscious users need a way to easily track their daily water intake because they may forget to drink water regularly, which results in difficulty maintaining a consistent hydration routine.

### **UX Problem Statement 2:**

Users need a way to receive timely water reminders because they may become busy and forget to drink water, which results in inconsistent water intake throughout the day.

## **2. Research Methods**

**User Interviews:**
To understand users' daily water-drinking habits, difficulties in tracking water intake, and reasons for forgetting to drink water.

**Surveys:**
To identify how frequently users drink water, whether they use reminders, and what features they expect from a health and fitness app.

**Usability Testing:**
To check whether users can easily understand their daily water goal, current water level, next reminder, and water-break notification.

## **3. User Personas**

**Age:** 19
**Occupation:** College Student

### **Demographics:**

* Has a busy daily schedule

### **Goals:**

* Track daily water intake.
* Reach the recommended daily water goal.
* Receive reminders to drink water.
* Maintain a healthy routine.

### **Behaviors:**

* Uses smartphone frequently.
* Often becomes busy with classes and assignments.
* Drinks water when he remembers.
* Prefers simple and quick health-tracking features.

### **Pain Points:**

* Frequently forgets to drink water.
* Does not track the amount of water consumed.
* Gets busy during college hours.
* Finds it difficult to maintain a regular hydration schedule.

### **Motivation:**

Wants a simple way to monitor his water intake and receive reminders so that he can maintain a healthy daily routine.

SCENARIO 2: RIDERS – BUS TRACKING APP
## **1. User Pain Points:**
Students and passengers may not know the current location of their bus.
Users may have to wait for the bus without knowing its arrival time.
Users may be confused about the stops covered by a particular route.
Users may not know which route the bus is following.
Users need quick information about upcoming bus stops.
Uncertain arrival times can cause unnecessary waiting and inconvenience.

## **UX Problem Statement 1:**

Bus passengers need a way to easily track their bus and view its expected arrival time because they cannot determine when the bus will reach their stop, which results in unnecessary waiting and uncertainty.

## **UX Problem Statement 2:**

Passengers need a way to clearly view the bus route and its stops because they may be unfamiliar with the route, which results in confusion and difficulty planning their journey.

## **2. Research Methods**

### **User Interviews:**
To understand passengers' problems with bus routes, waiting times, bus stops, and arrival information.

### **Surveys:**
To identify common transportation problems such as long waiting times, route confusion, and lack of real-time bus information.

### **Usability Testing:**
To check whether users can easily search for a route, understand the listed stops, identify the bus route, and find the expected arrival time.

## **3. User Personas**

### **Persona 1 – Daily Bus Passenger**

Name: Karthik
Age: 19
Occupation: College Student

### **Demographics:**
Age: 19
College student
Daily bus passenger
Smartphone user

### **Goals:**
Track the bus location.
Know the expected arrival time.
Identify the correct bus route.
Avoid unnecessary waiting.

### **Behaviors:**
Checks his phone while waiting for the bus.
Travels by bus regularly.
Searches for routes before travelling.
Prefers real-time transportation information.

### **Pain Points:**
Does not know when the bus will arrive.
Sometimes waits for a long time.
May be confused about bus routes and stops.
Cannot easily track the bus.

## **Motivation:**

Wants a simple and reliable way to track his bus and know exactly when it will arrive.

## **Persona 2 – Route Planning Passenger**

### **Pain Points:**
